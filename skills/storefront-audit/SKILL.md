# Storefront Audit / 店铺装修诊断

1. Capture the current customer-facing storefront and its observation time. Check whether the same merchant and menu variant are shown throughout.
2. Review navigation, featured products, names and portion information, image quality, and offer details. For each issue, record observed evidence and its customer impact.
3. Produce a proposed first screen using verified menu facts and approved source images. Prioritize discoverability and clarity. Keep uncertain claims out of customer-facing copy.
4. Provide before/after and an acceptance list. Confirm customers can find the featured item, distinguish categories, and understand the meal contents. If deployment is separately authorized, verify the receiving storefront and preserve rollback information.

## Known answer / 可核验样例

A fictional store shows 22 items under one category and a cropped hero image. The preview groups items under Signature Bowls, Sides and Drinks, shows the full hero dish and separates add-ons from included items. Review the preview against the fictional menu before publishing.
