# Competitive Merchant Sourcing / 竞对商圈与招商选品

1. Align market, category, active status, snapshot date and geographic coverage. Keep branches separate.
2. Match branch-level identities using location plus corroborating brand or listing evidence. Keep uncertain matches in a review queue. A matching brand name alone does not establish branch identity.
3. Count confirmed matches, review cases and verified gaps separately. Check product availability, reference price, currency, offer terms and funding before comparing discounts.
4. Rank candidates against explicit criteria such as category gap, verified discount and expected merchant contribution. Keep candidates with missing price evidence visible as evidence requests. Document the next sourcing action.

## Known answer / 可核对样例

North has four rival listings. One matches the reference catalog, one needs branch review and two are confirmed gaps in the synthetic catalog. Cedar Bowl is one gap with a CA$16 reference price and CA$12 offer. The discount is 25%. The CA$4 discount splits into CA$3 merchant funding and CA$1 platform funding. The other gap lacks reference-price evidence and stays pending.
