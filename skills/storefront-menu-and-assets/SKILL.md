# Whole-Store Menu and Assets / 整店菜单与营销物料

## Workflow / 流程

1. Convert the source menu into unique item IDs with category, bilingual names, variant and bundle components. Resolve combo references against the same draft.
2. Record each image or campaign asset with its intended item, combo or storefront-level placement.
3. Compare declared target and draft target before release. Inspect preview, naming, image and bundle content together. Escalate unresolved mapping and missing facts before an authorized publish.
4. After an authorized publish, verify the storefront-facing menu and retain the previous mapping.

## Known answer / 可核对样例

The fictional menu contains M01 (chicken bowl), M02 (cucumber salad), M03 (tea) and M04 (M01 + M03 combo). Menu artwork belongs to M01, the poster to M04 and the cover to the portfolio. Mapping the artwork to M02 yields exactly one mismatch.
